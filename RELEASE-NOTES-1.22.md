# FleshCraft 1.22

Everyone on a server needs the same version. The pack updates the mod by itself when the game starts.

## Changes
- Librarians sell enchanted books again. Pick the exact enchant, then add it at an anvil for one key.
- Crops can be planted in the dark; they only grow in light. Torches within 7 blocks of farmland never burn out.
- Temptress: spawns about half as often and drops less skin, but about 1 in 10 drops a diamond.
- Syringe fix: using your last syringe on a sheep now always gives Lamb's Blood (it used to vanish).
- Revive Syringe recipe: Lamb's Blood Syringe + Compound Gold Ingot + diamond.
- Rotten flesh on a lit campfire turns into Rancid Jerky; raw hearts cook into cooked hearts.
- The Nether rebuilds 2 minutes after the last player leaves it. Come back before then and nothing changes. While it rebuilds, the portal won't take you in.
- Bosses have a bit more health (+7.5%). Leviathan and The Tongue now show a boss bar.
- 1.22.1 boss buffs for 2-player fights: Scoutmaster +60% health and +15% damage, Temptress +20% health and +20% damage, The Ascetic +60% health and +20% damage, The Host +50% health, The Wailing Head +50% health, Heart of the Complex +35% damage.
- Boss deaths: no more red flash or tipping over, so each boss's own death animation plays cleanly.
- The corridors' hunter is fairer: it keeps away from Nether portals, gives a short warning before an out-of-sight first contact, calms down when everyone leaves, and no longer waits around near a portal you are standing at.
- Three new bad dreams (drowning, your home, the stairwell). Dreams cost no lives; getting caught leaves you hurt.
- The guide has been rewritten in short, practical tips.
- Shaders: Spooklementary is now on by default (Oculus).
- 1.22.2: Boss sites near spawn. Each boss that has a place in the world now has one within 1000 blocks of spawn (not on top of anyone's builds), and they show up on your Xaero's minimap and as waypoints by themselves when you join.
- 1.22.2: Fixed a server freeze when a player with several bases logged in (Ore Thief raid planning).
- 1.22.3-test (beta): early 1.22.3 test build (Fake House, boss anti-range counters, lag fixes) plus boss test kits (/devtest kit, needs cheats or op).
- 1.22.4: The Sunken Colossus now fights in a new hall, the Drowned Basilica.
- 1.22.4: New outsides for the Sunken Crypt, Butcher's Barn, Abandoned Church, Watchtower and Inn.
- 1.22.4: The Suckling has a new look, and when one latches onto your face you can now see it in first person.
- 1.22.4: The Witness now has one big bloodshot eye.
- 1.22.4: Beast fixes (smarter paths, no more getting stuck on glass, doors or tree canopies, steadier river crossings) and it now sometimes crawls up walls like a spider.
- 1.22.4: All bosses have 7% less health. The Amalgam calls in 35% fewer helpers.
- 1.22.4: Structure search no longer lags the server, and structures no longer overlap each other.
- 1.22.4: The world map planner now places all 18 boss sites.
- 1.22.5: New worlds are created much faster: boss planning takes about 36 seconds instead of about 11 minutes.
- 1.22.5: On fresh worlds all 18 bosses now get a site, including the Wailing Head.
- 1.22.6: Loading new chunks causes much less lag (about 76% fewer "can't keep up" stalls in our test).
- 1.22.6: The Flesh Pit and the Sinkhole no longer leak water or lava out of their walls.
- 1.22.6: The Drowned Basilica crypt is fully sealed.
- 1.22.6: The Beast no longer gets stuck in narrow doorways, goes through smashed glass, finds another climb route under overhangs, and follows you up raised slabs.
- 1.22.6: The Sunken Colossus can no longer be safely farmed from its feet or from high above: it stomps players at its feet and the current crushes players hovering above it.
- 1.22.6: The guide map's legend and status line now fit on the screen at every GUI scale.
- 1.22.6: The Marrow Spitter's hitbox now matches its body, so it is easier to hit, and it no longer suffocates when it climbs into a ceiling.
- 1.22.6: Fixed a missing-sound warning and some noisy server log lines.
- 1.22.7: The guide (J) now opens with "Your Goal: Nine Keys", has a YOUR GOAL box on the contents page, and a working Back button (Backspace also works). The book is bigger at small GUI scales.
- 1.22.7: The map in the guide no longer has a legend. Hover over an icon to see its name, its coordinates and how far away it is.
- 1.22.7: Drowned chapel roofs now sit on their walls. Abandoned churches no longer get cut into hillsides with floating terrain above them.
- 1.22.7: The Beast can't hit you right next to a Nether portal and leaves when you stand by one, no longer spins in place when you are very close, and climbs over walls instead of banging on them.
- 1.22.7: Better hitboxes: the Leviathan can be hit all the way to its tail, and The Host, the Hunter and the Cannibal Brute can be hit on their arms and upper body.
- 1.22.7: Server world regen: every boss site (plus the Sinkhole and The Door) can be planned within 1000 blocks of spawn, with a spruce forest ring around the kept base.
