# FleshCraft 1.22

Everyone on a server needs the same version. The pack updates the mod by itself when the game starts.

## Changes
- Librarians sell enchanted books again. Pick the exact enchant, then add it at an anvil for one key.
- Crops can be planted in the dark; they only grow in light. Torches within 7 blocks of farmland never burn out.
- Temptress: spawns about half as often and drops less skin, but about 1 in 10 drops a diamond.
- Syringe fix: using your last syringe on a sheep now always gives Lamb's Blood (it used to vanish).
- Revive Syringe recipe: Lamb's Blood Syringe + Compound Gold Ingot + diamond.
- Rotten flesh on a lit campfire turns into Rancid Jerky; raw hearts cook into cooked hearts.
- The Nether rebuilds 2 minutes after the last player leaves it. Come back before then and nothing changes. While it rebuilds, the portal won't take you in.
- Bosses have a bit more health (+7.5%). Leviathan and The Tongue now show a boss bar.
- 1.22.1 boss buffs for 2-player fights: Scoutmaster +60% health and +15% damage, Temptress +20% health and +20% damage, The Ascetic +60% health and +20% damage, The Host +50% health, The Wailing Head +50% health, Heart of the Complex +35% damage.
- Boss deaths: no more red flash or tipping over, so each boss's own death animation plays cleanly.
- The corridors' hunter is fairer: it keeps away from Nether portals, gives a short warning before an out-of-sight first contact, calms down when everyone leaves, and no longer waits around near a portal you are standing at.
- Three new bad dreams (drowning, your home, the stairwell). Dreams cost no lives; getting caught leaves you hurt.
- The guide has been rewritten in short, practical tips.
- Shaders: Spooklementary is now on by default (Oculus).
- 1.22.2: Boss sites near spawn. Each boss that has a place in the world now has one within 1000 blocks of spawn (not on top of anyone's builds), and they show up on your Xaero's minimap and as waypoints by themselves when you join.
- 1.22.2: Fixed a server freeze when a player with several bases logged in (Ore Thief raid planning).
- 1.22.3-test (beta): early 1.22.3 test build (Fake House, boss anti-range counters, lag fixes) plus boss test kits (/devtest kit, needs cheats or op).
- 1.22.4: The Sunken Colossus now fights in a new hall, the Drowned Basilica.
- 1.22.4: New outsides for the Sunken Crypt, Butcher's Barn, Abandoned Church, Watchtower and Inn.
- 1.22.4: The Suckling has a new look, and when one latches onto your face you can now see it in first person.
- 1.22.4: The Witness now has one big bloodshot eye.
- 1.22.4: Beast fixes (smarter paths, no more getting stuck on glass, doors or tree canopies, steadier river crossings) and it now sometimes crawls up walls like a spider.
- 1.22.4: All bosses have 7% less health. The Amalgam calls in 35% fewer helpers.
- 1.22.4: Structure search no longer lags the server, and structures no longer overlap each other.
- 1.22.4: The world map planner now places all 18 boss sites.
- 1.22.5: New worlds are created much faster: boss planning takes about 36 seconds instead of about 11 minutes.
- 1.22.5: On fresh worlds all 18 bosses now get a site, including the Wailing Head.
- 1.22.6: Loading new chunks causes much less lag (about 76% fewer "can't keep up" stalls in our test).
- 1.22.6: The Flesh Pit and the Sinkhole no longer leak water or lava out of their walls.
- 1.22.6: The Drowned Basilica crypt is fully sealed.
- 1.22.6: The Beast no longer gets stuck in narrow doorways, goes through smashed glass, finds another climb route under overhangs, and follows you up raised slabs.
- 1.22.6: The Sunken Colossus can no longer be safely farmed from its feet or from high above: it stomps players at its feet and the current crushes players hovering above it.
- 1.22.6: The guide map's legend and status line now fit on the screen at every GUI scale.
- 1.22.6: The Marrow Spitter's hitbox now matches its body, so it is easier to hit, and it no longer suffocates when it climbs into a ceiling.
- 1.22.6: Fixed a missing-sound warning and some noisy server log lines.
- 1.22.7: The guide (J) now opens with "Your Goal: Nine Keys", has a YOUR GOAL box on the contents page, and a working Back button (Backspace also works). The book is bigger at small GUI scales.
- 1.22.7: The map in the guide no longer has a legend. Hover over an icon to see its name, its coordinates and how far away it is.
- 1.22.7: Drowned chapel roofs now sit on their walls. Abandoned churches no longer get cut into hillsides with floating terrain above them.
- 1.22.7: The Beast can't hit you right next to a Nether portal and leaves when you stand by one, no longer spins in place when you are very close, and climbs over walls instead of banging on them.
- 1.22.7: Better hitboxes: the Leviathan can be hit all the way to its tail, and The Host, the Hunter and the Cannibal Brute can be hit on their arms and upper body.
- 1.22.7: Server world regen: every boss site (plus the Sinkhole and The Door) can be planned within 1000 blocks of spawn, with a spruce forest ring around the kept base.
- 1.22.8: The Sunken Colossus room has a new designed floor: a lit carpet from the entrance to the boss, compass roses, a shallow moat with bridges around the dais and a big rose under the Colossus.
- 1.22.8: The Sunken Colossus has a thicker body, and its hitboxes match. Standing beside its arms or head is no longer safe: it swats you there (watch for the red ring), and arrows and tridents fired from there do less damage.
- 1.22.8: The guide is readable at GUI scale 1. Every item you can get now has a guide entry (5 new pages), and entries for items that don't exist were removed.
- 1.22.8: Essence Syringe: same 2 minute buffs and +5 hearts, but you have to wait 10 minutes before the next one works.
- 1.22.8: Item balance: the Flesh Knight's limbo walls can't be mined; summoned Crucified stop getting stronger after the 10th; Pit tiers 1-3 have cooldowns; the Gravedigger's Shovel and Coat are weaker and drop 35% of the time; the Host's Carving Knife does 7 damage; the Icon of Strength gives x1.35 melee and wears off after 3 days; a Nether Static key is guaranteed by the 25th chest.
- 1.22.8: Buffs: the Flesh Knight's Helm gives armor and stops Bleed; the Scoutmaster's Hatchet swings faster and drops more often; the Shotgun can be repaired with Iron Ingots in an anvil. The Hunter's Bow guide page shows the right damage.
- 1.22.8: New outsides for the Dingy House, Hollow Village houses and the Overgrown Hospital (new worlds/new chunks only).
- 1.22.8: Linux: there is now a FleshCraft setup for Linux (fleshcraft-setup-linux).
- 1.22.8.1: With Hysteria shaders on, the Nether is now about as dark as with shaders off (pack-only update, same mod 1.22.8).
- 1.22.9: The Beast: the time between hunts is now random (30 s to 4.5 min alone; with friends 30 s to 3.5 min, and each player also gets a random break after being hunted), so you can't count down to the next hunt.
- 1.22.9: The Beast bangs on the walls much more often between hunts: random, slow, heavy knocks (1-4 at a time). It still never shows itself until it hunts.
- 1.22.9: Boss health bars no longer get stuck on screen: they disappear as soon as a boss dies or despawns, or when you go far away or change dimension.
- 1.22.9: Sunken Colossus: it no longer crushes you when you are above the crypt roof, at the surface, or in a boat. Players high up in its hall who are fighting it still get crushed, and the ink now comes from below.
- 1.22.9: Sunken Colossus hits 10% harder (all attacks).
- 1.22.9: Sunken Colossus spreads its attacks in multiplayer: slams, the key pull and its scavengers go after whoever it hasn't hit lately, not always the closest player. Anyone standing in reach still gets hit.
- 1.22.9: Sunken Colossus swat: the arm now sweeps across a red arc you can see building up (rising bubbles where the arm draws back, a whoosh before it swings). Step out of the red band to dodge; if it connects it throws you sideways along the swing.
- 1.22.10: The Beast: shorter random breaks between hunts. Alone: 30 s to 3 min. With friends: 30 s to 2 min 50 s, and each player's own break after being hunted is also 30 s to 2 min 50 s.
- 1.22.10: The Beast gets angry much faster: its aggression climbs from 100% to 200% over your first 20 minutes in the Nether (it used to take over 3 hours). Deeper layers still add more, and it calms down when everyone leaves the Nether.
- 1.22.10: The Beast's chases stretch to at most 1.5x at top aggression (was 1.75x).
- 1.22.10: The Beast only ever comes for you on its own schedule: spotting it or walking up to it outside a hunt never starts one.
- 1.22.11: The Beast's clap looks like a real clap now: it draws its arms back wide, swings them in and slams its palms together right in front of its face on the clap sound, then lets its arms drop. It stands still while it claps instead of running in place.
- 1.22.12: The Beast climbs better. When a roof or overhang blocks the top of a climb, it hangs on the wall and knocks the blocking blocks out with slow, heavy bangs (2 knocks, or 3 for hard blocks, about 1.3 s apart), then climbs on. If it can't break them, it finds another way instead of falling off and climbing again, and it no longer jumps or slips through blocks. Walls you built go down with one heavy bang per block about 0.75 s apart (a 2-thick 4-block cobblestone wall takes about 3 s), and doors and glass follow the same rule.
- 1.22.12: The Beast hunts a little more often on the first (top) Nether layer: the random break between hunts there is 30 s to 2 min 35 s alone (was 3 min) and 30 s to 2 min 25 s with friends, and each player's own break is 30 s to 2 min 25 s (was 2 min 50 s). The first hunt after you enter the Nether uses the same first-layer timing (it used to be 1 min to 2.5 min). Deeper layers keep 30 s to 3 min alone and 30 s to 2 min 50 s with friends. Each break is still at least 15 s longer or shorter than the one before, so it can't be timed.
- 1.22.13: The Beast hunts a little longer: each hunt now lasts 25 s to 1 min 15 s (was 20 s to 1 min). The breaks between hunts are unchanged.
- 1.22.13: The Titan's hitbox now follows its body when it falls over. While it is down or getting back up you can hit its head, back, legs and feet, and hits on the empty air above it no longer count. It still takes extra damage while it is down.
- 1.22.13: Structures no longer place wither skeleton skulls (the fallen power line and the Sunken Crypt front now use ordinary skeleton skulls). Wither skeleton skulls still come only from rare Nether chests.
- 1.22.13: Fetuses are now a good Pit offering: 12 points each (organs are 4, a Cannibal Eye is 6). The Harvest blessing still makes it count 1.5x.
- 1.22.13: Bosses now resist arrow spam (every boss except the Amalgam and the Wailing Head; the Titan and the Maw are included). About 1 in 4 arrows that hit a boss from the front clink off and do nothing. Each arrow in a quick volley does less damage, down to a quarter, and this wears off after about 4 seconds without being shot. After 6 arrows the boss's hide thickens and the boss glows with a red outline that everyone nearby can see: arrows, tridents and other shots bounce off until someone hits it up close, which tears the hide away and removes the outline. The outline never stays stuck and does not change the boss's look or teams afterwards. The Leviathan, the Tongue and the Ascetic also lose a thickened hide by themselves after 20 seconds. Arrows from far away still do at least about 15% of their normal damage each, even late in a volley (the long-range penalty and the volley penalty no longer stack below that). Melee damage is unchanged. This replaces the old rule where melee bosses below half health took half damage from arrows.
- 1.22.14: The Beast hunts a little more often. The random break between hunts is now 30 s to 2 min 20 s alone on the first (top) Nether layer (was 2 min 35 s) and 30 s to 2 min 10 s with friends (was 2 min 25 s). On the deeper layers it is 30 s to 2 min 40 s alone (was 3 min) and 30 s to 2 min 30 s with friends (was 2 min 50 s).
- 1.22.14: The Beast hunts a little longer, and the hunt clock only starts once it has reached you: each hunt lasts 30 s to 1 min 25 s (was 25 s to 1 min 15 s), counted from when it first gets within 20 blocks of its target. If it never gets that close, the clock starts anyway after 1 minute. Leaving the Nether, dying or disconnecting still ends a hunt straight away.
- 1.22.14: New Beast screams. A hunt now opens with one of three screams, picked at random and never the same one twice in a row. While it chases you it screams every 6 to 12 seconds, it roars when it is walled off or can't find a way to you, and it shrieks when it breaks through a wall or door. Between hunts you may hear it far off in the dark, from a direction that isn't where it really is (never during a hunt). The old chase screams are replaced. All new sounds are public-domain (CC0) recordings from Freesound.
- 1.22.14: After the opening scream the Beast now reaches you in about 8 to 20 seconds, usually around 12. It waits out of sight first instead of being on you instantly, and it never appears in front of you.
- 1.22.14: Fix: before it has reached you, outrunning the Beast no longer ends the hunt early; it moves out of sight and comes back for you. If it truly cannot reach you (sealed in), the hunt still ends as before.
- 1.22.14: Fixes: a Watcher's Eye no longer outlines the Beast through walls while it is hidden; the "There's Something Else Here" advancement now needs you to actually see the Beast; when the Beast breaks into a sealed room its opening scream no longer plays on top of the break-through shriek.
- 1.22.15: The Beast never reaches you sooner than 8 seconds after its opening scream, even if you run toward it. If it gets close early, it stops and stares until the time is up.
- 1.22.15: Sealing yourself in obsidian or other blast-proof blocks (crying obsidian, ancient debris, netherite, reinforced deepslate, ender chests) no longer keeps the Beast out. It bangs through them slowly. Bedrock and the world border still stop it.
- 1.22.15: Hiding in a one-block-high crawl space no longer keeps the Beast out. It breaks into the space.
- 1.22.15: Shields no longer block the Beast forever. Its swipe knocks your shield out, like an axe does.
- 1.22.15: Logging out during a hunt no longer gets rid of the Beast. When you log back in inside the Nether, it comes for you again within 10 to 20 seconds.
- 1.22.15: Fix: while the Beast is hidden, your game is no longer told where it is, and you can no longer hit it, bump into it or target it.
- 1.22.15: Fix: a rare server crash when the Nether rebuilds itself (a jack o'lantern placed next to an unloaded area) no longer happens.
- 1.22.16: If you switch to creative or spectator mode during a hunt, the Beast stops hunting you.
