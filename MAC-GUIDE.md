# How to play FleshCraft on a Mac

Takes about 10 minutes. You need a Microsoft account that owns **Minecraft: Java Edition**.

**Do NOT use CurseForge.** It won't work, so just close it.

---

## Step 1. Download it

Click this link:

### 👉 [Download FleshCraft for Mac](https://github.com/fuckitscomingout/magiclives-pack/releases/latest/download/FleshCraft-Setup-Mac.zip)

Wait for the download to finish.

---

## Step 2. Open your Downloads

1. Click the **Finder** icon (the blue smiley face at the bottom of your screen).
2. On the left side, click **Downloads**.
3. Find **FleshCraft-Setup-Mac.zip**.
4. **Double-click it.** A new thing called **FleshCraft Setup** appears next to it.

---

## Step 3. Open FleshCraft Setup

**Double-click FleshCraft Setup.**

Your Mac will probably show a scary box that says **"FleshCraft Setup" Not Opened**.

- ✅ Click **Done**.
- ❌ Do **NOT** click "Move to Trash".

That box is normal. It's safe. Go to Step 4.

(If the Mac just asks "Are you sure you want to open it?", click **Open** and skip to Step 5.)

---

## Step 4. Tell your Mac it's OK

1. Click the **Apple logo** in the very top-left corner of your screen.
2. Click **System Settings**.
3. On the left side, click **Privacy & Security**.
4. Scroll **all the way down** on the right side.
5. You'll see **"FleshCraft Setup" was blocked**. Click **Open Anyway**.
6. Type your Mac password (the one you use to unlock your Mac) and press Enter.
7. If another box pops up, click **Open** (or **Open Anyway**).

---

## Step 5. Let it install

The setup now does everything by itself. Just wait. Don't close anything.

When it's done, a window called **Prism Launcher** opens.

---

## Step 6. Sign in (first time only)

1. Prism Launcher asks you to sign in. Click to add a **Microsoft** account.
2. Sign in with the Microsoft account you use for Minecraft.
3. When it's done, click **FleshCraft**, then click **Launch**.

The game opens and joins the FleshCraft server by itself. 🎉

---

## Next time you want to play

Open **Prism Launcher**, click **FleshCraft**, then click **Launch**. It updates by itself every time.

---

## Something went wrong?

**I didn't see "Open Anyway" in Step 4.**
1. Press **Command + Space**, type **Terminal**, and press Enter.
2. Copy this whole line, paste it into Terminal, and press Enter:
   ```
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/"FleshCraft Setup.app"
   ```
3. Close Terminal and go back to Step 3.

**I got an error that says "manifest.json".**
You put it into CurseForge. Close CurseForge and start again from Step 1.
